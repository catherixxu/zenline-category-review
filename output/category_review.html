How to navigate

  • Use the tabs at the top to move between sections. Inside Actions, pick an action by its keyword.
  • Actions are numbered in the order to carry them out: fix the range you have, then change it, then grow it.
  • Hover over a product row or a chart mark to see its full figures.
  • Products marked "Check first" have an open data point. The marker takes you to it.
  • To print or save as PDF, use the print function of your browser. All tabs print in order.

How to read the figures

  • Euro figures are estimates for a 12-week period. They measure different things, so they are shown side by side and not added up.
  • Each product appears under one action only.
  • "Provisional" marks an estimate that depends on open data points.
  • The product list is also available as action_list.csv. Assumptions and calculations are documented in assumptions_and_calculations.md. Both sit next to this file.

How is the sales trend calculated?All five actions use the trend as a direction. A different basis could move products across the thresholds.
Do inactive products still occupy the shelf space listed for them?If they do, relisting needs no new space and action 3 is not a precondition for action 4.
Is there a minimum revenue per product per week?It would replace the bottom-quarter cut-off used for delisting in action 3 with the retailer's own standard.
