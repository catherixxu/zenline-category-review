How to read the estimates

  • All estimates are simple 12-week run rates. They assume no change in volume when a price changes and no effect of one action on another.
  • Sales trends are used as a direction only. No euro figure is derived from a trend.
  • Each product has one primary action. Where several rules apply, the order is restock, relist, promote, price review, delist.
  • Competitor data is matched by exact product name only, which is why just 2 price gaps are shown.

Open questions for ZenBeauty

  • What does the status "inaktiv" mean in practice? We read it as being phased out while the remaining stock sells down.
  • How is the sales trend calculated? We use it as a direction only and never derive euro figures from it.
  • Do inactive products still occupy the shelf space listed for them? If they do, relisting needs no new space.
  • Is there a minimum revenue per product per week that should replace the bottom-quarter cut-off used for delisting?
